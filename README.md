# MiNMiNTV channel logos

Our own copy of the channel logos used by the MiNMiNTV playlist lineup, so the app does not depend on third-party hosts.

- countries/canada, countries/united-states: copied from https://github.com/tv-logo/tv-logos at commit d32e347bb7c4c640dceec23957802ad9182f58a6 (plus the few single files from other countries the lineup uses).
- misc/24-7: the 24/7 channel logos from the same repository.
- imported/: logos matched from the iptv-org database (https://github.com/iptv-org/api) and from the EPG guide icons.

Logos are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the channels. Files are served through jsDelivr at a pinned commit.
